About the Academy

A quiet, disciplined place to learn the Qur'an

Al Irshad was founded on a simple belief: that learning to recite and understand the Qur'an properly deserves the same seriousness as any other lifelong skill. We pair every student with a teacher suited to their level, age, and pace — not a fixed curriculum forced onto everyone.

Whether you are a young child starting with Noorani Qaida, an adult returning to Tajweed after years away, or a hafiz-in-progress working toward memorization, our teachers meet you where you are.

What We Teach

Courses for every stage

01

Noorani Qaida

The foundation course — Arabic letters, correct pronunciation (makharij), and joining letters, built for absolute beginners of any age.

02

Qur'an Reading (Nazra)

Fluent, correct recitation of the full Qur'an from cover to cover with a focus on accuracy and confidence.

03

Tajweed Rules

The science of correct pronunciation — makharij, sifaat, and the rules that transform reading into proper recitation.

04

Hifz-ul-Qur'an

Structured, teacher-guided memorization of the full Qur'an with daily revision (sabaq, sabqi, manzil).

05

Islamic Studies

Aqeedah, Fiqh essentials, Seerah, and daily du'as — grounding recitation in understanding.

06

Arabic Language

Conversational and Qur'anic Arabic, so students can eventually read the Qur'an's meaning directly.

Our Method

How a student progresses with us

  1. 1

    Free Assessment

    A short trial class where the teacher listens to the student read and places them at the right starting point.

  2. 2

    Personalized Plan

    A weekly schedule built around the student's age, goals, and available time — not a one-size-fits-all syllabus.

  3. 3

    Live, One-to-One Classes

    Regular sessions with the same teacher throughout, so progress is tracked and corrected consistently.

  4. 4

    Monthly Progress Report

    Parents and adult students receive a clear report on what's been covered and what's next.
